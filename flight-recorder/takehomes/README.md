# Take-homes: free, vendor-neutral, usable Monday

Nothing here is for sale, and no product or vendor is named. Copy, edit, and use these in your own organization under the MIT license. No sign-up, no email, no follow-up call.

| Take-home | What it is | Use it right away |
|---|---|---|
| [Permission card template](permission-card-template.md) | One page that says what an AI agent may do, may never do, and who answers for it | Fill it out for one AI workflow you already run, and get the business owner to sign it |
| [Pilot to production checklist](pilot-to-production-checklist.md) | 10 yes/no questions before an AI workflow goes live | Run it in your next AI steering or risk meeting |
| [Curveball checklist](curveball-checklist.md) | 27 real-world data traps (handwriting, time zones, rounding, look-alike names, hidden text) with public sources | Test your own pipeline against five of them this week |
| [Four gateways labs](../../dama-2026/) | Runnable Python examples of each control, plus an AGENTS.md and guardrails.yaml you can adapt | Copy the AGENTS.md into your own agent project and rewrite it for your rules |

All demo data is synthetic sample data. Regulatory references point to public text; check your own state's adopted version. Teaching example, not legal advice.
