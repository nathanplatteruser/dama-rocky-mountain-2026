Run it on your laptop

Free local

Mock mode needs nothing. Ollama keeps everything on your laptop, $0.

cd dama-2026
python3 run_all.py
FR_BACKEND=ollama FR_MODEL=llama3.1:8b python3 run_all.py

Bring your own key

Claude, Grok, a ChatGPT model, or any OpenAI-compatible server. Redacted synthetic data only; the code refuses to send raw sensitive values. Your key stays in your terminal session and is never saved. Roughly 1 to 15 cents a run.

cd dama-2026
export ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=...   # your own key
FR_BACKEND=anthropic python3 run_all.py
# or FR_BACKEND=xai / openai, pick with FR_MODEL
